## Hello everybody 👋

My name is Carsten and I'm a professional software developer and computer graphics researcher. I am mostly writing computer graphics and rendering software. I love video game technology. 

In the past, I also did a lot of modding for the GTA III era games. I started and lead the [GTA: United](http://www.gtaunited.net/home.p1.html) modification for GTA: San Andreas for most of it's development time and did some programming and scripting for [GTA: Berlin](http://www.gtaberlin.de/). I also wrote some related modding tools and contributed to the [GTA Mods Wiki](https://gtamods.com/wiki/Main_Page).

In my research I focus on Virtual and Mixed Reality. In my free-time I study graphics engine architecture and modern rendering APIs. In particular, I am developing the [LiteFX engine](https://litefx.crudolph.io).
